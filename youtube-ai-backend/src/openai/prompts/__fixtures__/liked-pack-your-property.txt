=== 1. PACK YOUR PROPERTY ===
Imagine lying on your bunk—
finally feeling familiar with the place.
Then you hear—
“Pack your property. You’re leaving.”
Your stomach tightens.
Leaving doesn’t mean going home.

[BEAT]

Diddy’s been transferred.
I spent twenty-six years inside.
Let me put you—
in the position we’re examining.
What does starting over—
take out of a man?

[PAUSE]

You learned one routine.
Now picture another institution.
Different surroundings.
People you haven’t met.
And no option to say—
“This ain’t for me.”

[BEAT]

Meanwhile, imagine your family—
looking down at a phone.
They want one clear conversation.
You want to reassure them.
Neither side can solve—
that distance by wanting harder.

[BEAT]

I’m not describing Diddy’s private experience.
I’m putting the consequence—
where you can feel it.
Because the danger ain’t—
just something happening around you.
It’s losing control of your life.

[BEAT]

We know his new destination.
But before we discuss—
what starting over can mean—
there’s something we must separate.
Where he went—
from why he went.


=== JEWEL ===
A different address isn’t freedom.
You can move without moving forward.
What are you carrying with you?

=== 3. I KNOW WHAT DISTANCE COSTS ===
I did twenty-six years.
Lompoc.
Atwater.
Victorville.
Far from my people—
back home in New York.

[BEAT]

And let me own something.
I was still making decisions—
I had no business making.
Still trying to get money.
Still complicating my time.
That part belongs to me.

[BEAT]

From where I stood—
distance limited my access—
to my New York base.
That’s how I understood it.
I cannot take my experience—
and call it Diddy’s transfer reason.

[BEAT]

But I know what—
those miles meant to me.
More travel for a visit.
More money.
More time somebody needed—
away from their own responsibilities.

[BEAT]

Sometimes love was there—
but the person couldn’t be.
I could understand the reason.
Still didn’t fill that chair.


=== JEWEL ===
Accountability includes the people who stayed.
Your decisions can spend their resources.
Who helped pay for your time?

This is my analysis.

without making your situation worse.

[BEAT]

[BEAT]

[BEAT]

the responsibility in front of you.
Getting better matters—
more than looking powerful.

Practice control over yourself.

Go back to your home.

Who depends on—
you being available?

[PAUSE]

Not your reputation.

You.
Present for ordinary things.
A ride.
A difficult conversation.

[BEAT]

We don’t know—

[BEAT]

That’s why I tell you—

=== 7. DON’T TURN PRISON INTO A TEST ===
Let me be clear.
I’m not predicting—
Diddy gets attacked at Allenwood.
I’m not calling—
this institution worse for him.
We haven’t established that.

[BEAT]

The warning doesn’t need—

Losing your choices—

Don’t make suffering sound exciting.

[BEAT]

My advice is restraint.
Respect boundaries.
Follow the rules.
Use legitimate channels—
when there’s a problem.
Build something productive into your day.

[BEAT]

And if you’re outside—
don’t wait for confinement—
to practice those things.
Walk away from foolishness.
Get help with your anger.
Stop auditioning for reckless people.


=== JEWEL ===
Survival isn’t the goal to chase.
Prevention can protect the life you have.
Why insist on learning through loss?
